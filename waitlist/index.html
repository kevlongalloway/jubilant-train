§ i — On why the margin disappeared

Every book you've ever read was already
full of other people's thoughts.
The underlines. The question marks. The bracket
around a sentence that changed how someone lived.

We stripped those away.

Digital reading arrived and decided that "clean"
meant empty — no margins, no conversation,
no trace that anyone had been here before.1

The margin was not wasted space. It was the conversation.

Précis is the margin, returned.2  A network
where the annotation is the unit of thought — not
the post, not the star rating, not the hot take.
The moment you stop and write: wait. this.

Bring your books. Bring your arguments.3
Find the three readers who mark up the
same passage as you — and disagree.
Talk it through across the margin.

founding invitation

We're building with a small cohort first —
readers and writers who want a place that
takes the text as seriously as the conversation
around it.4 Leave your name in the margin.
